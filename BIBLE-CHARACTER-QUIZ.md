# Bible Quiz - Complete Flow Guide (The Star Followers)
## FUN & SILLY VERSION — 20 Characters + Dimension Scoring
## TRIMMED FOR 4-6 MINUTE COMPLETION

> Self-aware narration + exaggerated characters. Same structure, same scoring, way more personality.

---

## Story Summary

A suspiciously bright star appears in the sky. Against your better judgment, you join a group of strangers who've decided to just... follow it. No map. No itinerary. No refund policy. The journey will test your patience, your snack supply, and your ability to handle weird encounters with even weirder people. But somehow, you know you're supposed to be here.

---

## How Scoring Works

Each choice adds points to 5 personality dimensions. At the end, your dimension scores are matched to the nearest character using a weighted distance algorithm. **Results are gender-filtered** — women see women characters, men see men characters.

### The 5 Personality Dimensions

| Dimension | Spectrum | What It Measures |
|-----------|----------|------------------|
| **Action Style** | Bold ↔ Steady | How you respond when something needs to happen |
| **Social Focus** | People ↔ Purpose | What drives your decisions — relationships or mission? |
| **Leadership Mode** | Front ↔ Support | Do you step forward or strengthen from behind? |
| **Crisis Response** | Intervene ↔ Endure | When trouble hits, do you act or trust the process? |
| **Spiritual Expression** | Contemplative ↔ Demonstrative | Is your faith quiet and internal or vocal and visible? |

## Quiz Structure Overview

The quiz starts at **Scene 1** and branches into 4 main paths at **Scene 2**:

1. **Valley Path** (Scene 3) → Woman at well → Starving village → Shepherds → Final section
2. **Hills Path** (Scene 4) → Hermit cave → Injured companion → Summit → Final section
3. **River Path** (Scene 5) → Children/Grandmother → Flooded crossing → Final section
4. **Wilderness Path** (Scene 6) → Abandoned camp → Farmer → Final section

All paths converge at **Scene 19** (Checkpoint) → **Scene 22** (Stable) → **Scene 13** (Final Question)
