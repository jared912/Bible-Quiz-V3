## CARD TEMPLATE STRUCTURE

```
[HEADER]
Who You Are:
[Name] — [Archetype]

[MAIN SECTION]
- Character Illustration (placeholder)
- Scripture Reference
- Strengths (3 bullet points)
- Weaknesses (3 bullet points)

[QUOTES SECTION]
- 2 signature phrases this character would say

[BOTTOM SECTION]
- Hidden Talent (one line)
- Peer Reviews (2 quotes from biblical figures)
```

## 1. DEBORAH
**The Courageous Leader**

### Scripture Reference
Judges 4-5

### Strengths:
- Natural leader who people trust with their actual lives
- Somehow stays calm when everyone else is panicking
- Will speak hard truth AND make you feel respected

### Weaknesses:
- Has accidentally intimidated someone just by entering a room
- Doesn't understand why people need so much reassurance
- Forgets that not everyone has her confidence

### Things Deborah Would Say:
> "I'll go with you, but you need to actually get up first."

> "We don't have time for a committee meeting. Let's move."

### Hidden Talent:
Making terrifying situations feel manageable through sheer presence.

### Peer Reviews:
> "She told me to go to battle and I just... went. That's the effect she has."
> — **Barak**

> "When Deborah speaks, you listen. Not because she's loud — because she's right."
> — **Miriam**

> "She sat under a palm tree and nations came to her. That's authority."
> — **The elders of Israel**

## 2. ESTHER
**The Strategic Protector**

### Scripture Reference
Esther 4-7

### Strengths:
- Knows exactly when to speak and when to wait
- Can read a room better than anyone you know
- Brave in a way that doesn't need to be loud

### Weaknesses:
- Overthinks the timing of everything (even text messages)
- Carries the weight of other people's problems silently
- Has a backup plan for her backup plan

### Things Esther Would Say:
> "If I'm going to do this, I'm going to do it right."

> "Give me three days. I have a plan."

### Hidden Talent:
Turning impossible situations around through patience and perfect timing.

### Peer Reviews:
> "She risked everything for us. And she made it look effortless."
> — **Mordecai**

> "Strategic? Yes. Cold? Never. That's what makes her dangerous."
> — **Abigail**

> "Born for a time such as this."
> — **The Jewish people**

## 3. ABIGAIL
**The Wise Peacemaker**

### Scripture Reference
1 Samuel 25

### Strengths:
- Can de-escalate any conflict with the right words at the right time
- Genuinely wise — not just smart, actually wise
- Will protect people from their own bad decisions

### Weaknesses:
- Sometimes fixes problems that weren't hers to fix
- Has definitely apologized for someone else's behavior
- Takes on emotional labor like it's a competitive sport

### Things Abigail Would Say:
> "Let me handle this before it becomes a whole thing."

> "He didn't mean it like that. Probably."

### Hidden Talent:
Preventing disasters that no one else even saw coming.

### Peer Reviews:
> "She saved my life by telling me I was wrong. I married her."
> — **David**

> "The smartest person in most rooms, and somehow the most humble."
> — **Esther**

> "Wisdom in a crisis. Grace under pressure."
> — **Her servants**

## 4. PRISCILLA
**The Invested Teacher**

### Scripture Reference
Acts 18; Romans 16

### Strengths:
- Will actually take time to explain things properly
- Sees potential in people before they see it in themselves
- Builds others up without needing any credit

### Weaknesses:
- Gets too invested in other people's growth (it keeps her up at night)
- Has corrected someone "gently" and it was still a lot
- Forgets to take breaks because someone needs mentoring

### Things Priscilla Would Say:
> "You're not wrong, but let me show you a better way."

> "I'm not doing this FOR you, I'm doing this WITH you."

### Hidden Talent:
Making people feel smart while actually teaching them everything.

### Peer Reviews:
> "She and Aquila took me aside and I left a different person. Grateful."
> — **Apollos**

> "Everyone needs a Priscilla in their corner."
> — **Paul**

> "Taught without ego. Corrected without condescension. Rare then. Rare now."
> — **Aquila**

## 6. RUTH
**The Loyal Companion**

### Scripture Reference
Ruth 1-4

### Strengths:
- Will literally follow you to another country if you need support
- Somehow makes "quiet and dependable" feel like a superpower
- The friend who remembers your coffee order from three years ago

### Weaknesses:
- Has trouble putting herself first (will starve so you can eat)
- Takes "ride or die" a little too literally

### Things Ruth Would Say:

> "I'm not being clingy, I'm being *committed*."

### Hidden Talent:
Making you feel like the most important person in the room without saying a word.

### Peer Reviews:
> "She stayed when everyone else left. That's not normal loyalty. That's Ruth."
> — **Naomi**

> "The kind of person you want in your corner forever."
> — **Hannah**

> "Loyalty that crosses borders. Love that outlasts loss."
> — **Boaz**

### Hidden Talent:
Turning personal pain into something that blesses other people.

### Peer Reviews:
> "She prayed me into existence and then gave me back to God. That's my mother."
> — **Samuel**

> "I've never seen faith like hers. Quiet, stubborn, unshakeable."
> — **Ruth**

> "Prayed when she had nothing. Gave back when she finally had something. Faith like that changes history."
> — **Eli**

## 10. MARTHA
**The Practical Caregiver**

### Scripture Reference
Luke 10; John 11

### Strengths:
- Gets things DONE while everyone else is still talking
- Shows love through actions, not just words
- Will feed you whether you asked or not

### Weaknesses:
- Has been stressed about hosting at least once this week
- Lowkey resentful when no one helps (but won't say it directly)
- Thinks "resting" is just sitting while making a mental to-do list

### Hidden Talent:
Making everyone feel cared for through acts of service they didn't even ask for.

### Peer Reviews:
> "She was upset about the dishes, but she also told me she believed I was the Messiah. Both are true."
> — **Jesus**

> "My sister does too much. But she does it because she loves hard."
> — **Mary of Bethany**

> "Served when others sat. Spoke when others stayed silent. Believed when it mattered most."
> — **Lazarus**

## 11. MOSES
**The Reluctant Leader**

### Scripture Reference
Exodus 3-4, 14, 32

### Strengths:
- Steps up when it matters, even when terrified
- Advocates for people who can't advocate for themselves
- Actually listens to God (eventually)

### Weaknesses:
- Will give you five reasons why someone else should do it
- Has anger management moments he's not proud of
- Overcomplicates simple instructions

### Things Moses Would Say:
> "I'm not a good speaker, but fine, I'LL DO IT."

> "Please send someone else. No? Okay, give me the staff."

### Hidden Talent:
Leading millions of people while convinced he's unqualified.

### Peer Reviews:
> "He didn't want the job. That's exactly why he was right for it."
> — **Aaron**

> "Eighty years old and just getting started. Late bloomers, take note."
> — **Joshua**

> "Stubborn with Pharaoh. Stubborn with God. Stubborn enough to free us all."
> — **Miriam**

## 13. ELIJAH
**The Bold Prophet**

### Scripture Reference
1 Kings 17-19

### Strengths:
- Will confront anyone, regardless of their title or power
- Completely unintimidated by popular opinion
- Stands alone when no one else will

### Hidden Talent:
Being supernaturally bold and emotionally exhausted at the same time.

### Peer Reviews:
> "He called fire from heaven. Then he needed a nap and a snack. Both are valid."
> — **God**

> "My mentor. Intense doesn't begin to cover it."
> — **Elisha**

> "A man fully used by God."
> — **The prophets**

# DESIGNER NOTES

## Card Dimensions
- **Instagram Story**: 1080 x 1920 px
- **Instagram Feed**: 1080 x 1080 px (may need condensed version)

## Visual Style Suggestions
- Clean, modern, NOT overly "churchy"
- Bold colors that pop on social media
- Illustration style: Simple and iconic (like vegetal quiz)
- Gender-coded color schemes optional (or unified brand colors)

## Required Elements
- Quiz URL/branding at bottom
- "Who You Are:" header
- Character illustration placeholder

## Optional Elements
- "Share your result!" prompt
- "I got [Character]!" overlay text for sharing

## Color Palette Suggestions
Could assign signature colors:
- **Women**: Warm tones (Ruth = terracotta, Esther = deep purple, etc.)
- **Men**: Cool tones (Peter = ocean blue, David = forest green, etc.)
- Or unified brand palette across all 20
