# Bible Quiz - Complete Flow Guide (The Star Followers)
## REVISED VERSION

> This document shows every scene, choice, and path in the quiz.

---

## Story Summary

A brilliant star appears in the night sky. You join a group of travelers following it across unfamiliar lands. The journey tests your character through encounters with strangers, dangers, and difficult choices. In the end, you arrive at a humble stable where a baby cries - and you realize you were meant to be here.

---

## Quiz Structure Overview

The quiz starts at **Scene 1** and branches into 4 main paths at **Scene 2**:

1. **Valley Path** (Scene 3) → Woman at well → Starving village → Shepherds → Final section
2. **Hills Path** (Scene 4) → Hermit cave → Injured companion → Summit → Final section
3. **River Path** (Scene 5) → Children/Grandmother → Flooded crossing → Final section
4. **Wilderness Path** (Scene 6) → Abandoned camp → Farmer → Final section

All paths converge at **Scene 19** (Checkpoint) → **Scene 22** (Stable) → **Scene 13** (Final Question)
